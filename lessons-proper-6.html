Learning Numbers

Numbers help us count objects, tell how many things we have, and understand quantities. Let's learn how to recognize and count numbers!

Counting Objects

We use numbers to count objects around us. Here are some examples of objects that we can count.

There are three apples.

There is one pencil.

There are four erasers.

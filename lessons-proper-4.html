Learn About Weather

Have you ever noticed it could be sunny today, but rainy tomorrow? Let's learn about different weather conditions. What's the weather like at your area right now?

Weather

The weather is what the air and sky is doing right now at a specific place. It can change fast from a bright sunny morning to a dark rainy afternoon. There are five common types of weather: sunny, cloudy, windy, rainy, and stormy.

Sunny

Sunny weather happens when the sun is shining brightly outside and the sky is clear. The day is warm and bright. It's a perfect time to play, but make sure to stay hydrated and put on sunscreen.

Cloudy

Cloudy weather happens when the sky if covered with clouds. The sun may be hidden behind these clouds, which makes the day look dull and less bright. Cloudy weather can also mean that rain is coming soon.

Windy

Windy weather happens when the air around us moves quickly. During windy weather, leaves and tree branches move with the wind. If you're walking outside, using an umbrella might be difficult as your umbrella can catch the wind. Be sure to hold on to your hat so that it doesn't fly away!

Rainy

Rainy weather is when water from the clouds falls to the ground. We use umbrellas and raincoats to keep ourselves dry. It is best to stay indoors during the rain to keep ourselves from getting sick.

Stormy

Stormy weather happens when there are strong winds, heavy rain, and sometimes even thunder and lightning happening all at once. They can be dangerous, so we should all stay indoors and listen to the instructions from adults.

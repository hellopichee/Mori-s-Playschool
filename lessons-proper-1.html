Different Kinds of Animals

In this lesson, Mori aims to teach playmates how to identify animals in houses, farms, and the wild.

Animals in Houses

Animals that people keep in their houses are called pets. They can be our best friends because they bring us joy. We must take care of them by giving them food, clean water, shelter, and love. Do you have a pet in your home? Examples of pets are dogs, cats, guinea pigs, and goldfish.

Animals in the Farm

Farm animals are animals that live on farms. Some give us food, such as milk and eggs, while others help farmers do their work. Just like pets, they also need proper care, food, and a safe place to live. Examples of farm animals include cows, chickens, pigs, goats, and horses.

Animals in the Wild

Wild animals live in nature, such as forests, grasslands, and jungles. They find their own food and shelter in the wild without any help from people. That is why we should respect them and let them live safely in their natural homes. Examples of wild animals include elephants, lions, tigers, pandas, and monkeys (like Mori!)

Test Yourself!

1. Which of these animals is a house pet?

Show Answer

Dogs are common house pets, They can live with people and be their companions.

2. Which of these animals is a farm animal?

Show Answer

Pigs are often raised in farms. They love exploring and digging with their snouts.

3. Which of these animals is a wild animal?

Show Answer

Lions are wild animals that live in grasslands and savannas. They are known for their strength and loud roars.
